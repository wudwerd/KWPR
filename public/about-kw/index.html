Building brands.
Shaping reputations.

KW PR is a London-based communications agency working internationally across wellness and fashion. We develop the stories, relationships and experiences that bring brands and the people behind them into focus.

Our work combines strategic positioning with media relations, talent partnerships and events. From a founder profile to a brand relaunch, we connect each part of a campaign around a clear story and a shared commercial objective.

Led by Chris Woodward, whose experience spans 20 years in PR, KW brings together media, talent and specialist partners around each brief. We shape the strategy and coordinate delivery, from initial planning through to coverage and follow-up.
